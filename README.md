# Evil Crawling Thing

> A procedural spider thing for Garry's Mod that hunts, grabs and eats.

On the [Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818771209).

## Needs

- [Syhgma's Evil API](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823740816) ([source](https://github.com/xandwrp/gmod-evil-api))

## Spawning

Entities > Syhgma's Evil Entities.

- Evil Crawler goes where you point
- Evil Crawler (somewhere) turns up somewhere nobody can see

## What it does

- Walks on IK legs over floors, walls and ceilings
- Tracks you by sight, sound and scent, and goes to where it last saw you
- Opens doors, squeezes through vents and tears through props when chasing
- Grabbed players can mash to fight free
- Eats corpses when hungry, and can grow with every body
- Runs when hurt, snaps after enough hits, and can be knocked out and killed
- NPCs shoot at it

## Settings

Spawn menu > Utilities > Syhgma's Evil Entities > Evil Crawling Thing (superadmins), or the convars. They all start with `evil_crawler_`.
